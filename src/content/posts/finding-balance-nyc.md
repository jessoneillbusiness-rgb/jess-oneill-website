New York will take everything you give it. Your time, your energy, your sanity — if you let it. I spent my first year here running on adrenaline and coffee, convinced that slowing down meant falling behind.

I was wrong.

## The Wake-Up Call

Burnout doesn't announce itself. It creeps in — the irritability, the exhaustion that sleep doesn't fix, the joy leaking out of things you used to love. For me, it was realizing I hadn't read a book for pleasure in six months.

## What Changed

**Morning pages.** Ten minutes of journaling before I touch my phone. Non-negotiable.

**Neighborhood walks.** Not for content, not for steps — just to notice my block. The bodega cat. The new mural. The old man who feeds pigeons at the same time every day.

**Saying no.** To events, to collaborations that don't align, to the FOMO that NYC manufactures.

**A real Sunday.** No work. Farmers market, a long bath, cooking something that takes two hours.

## The Abundance Mindset

Living abundantly isn't about having more — it's about being fully present for what you already have. This city offers infinite experiences, but you don't need to collect them all.

You just need to savor the ones that matter.
