Miami has a way of pulling you in from the moment you land. The humidity, the pastel buildings, the rhythm of the city — it all feels like stepping into a different world.

## Day One: South Beach & Wynwood

Start your morning with a cortadito at **Versailles** (yes, the iconic Cuban spot — worth every minute of the wait). Then head to South Beach for a stroll along Ocean Drive before the crowds arrive.

By afternoon, make your way to Wynwood Walls. The street art here is constantly evolving, and you'll want at least two hours to wander, photograph, and grab an açaí bowl from one of the nearby cafés.

> Miami isn't a city you rush through. It's a city you feel.

## Day Two: Little Havana & Sunset

Little Havana on Calle Ocho is non-negotiable. Watch the domino players at Máximo Gómez Park, browse the fruit stands, and end with dinner at a family-run restaurant where the mojitos are strong and the ropa vieja is unforgettable.

Catch sunset from a rooftop bar in Brickell — the skyline against that golden hour light is pure magic.

## What to Pack

- Lightweight linen everything
- SPF 50 (reapply obsessively)
- A good camera or phone with plenty of storage
- Comfortable walking sandals

Until next time, Miami. You always deliver.
