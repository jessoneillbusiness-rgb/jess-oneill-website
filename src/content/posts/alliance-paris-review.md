If you are picking one Michelin restaurant in Paris and you want it to feel modern rather than stiff, go to [Alliance](https://www.restaurant-alliance.fr/en/). I had the seven-course menu, they kept sending extra plates because the kitchen was excited about them, and the caviar is the thing I still think about.

It is chef Toshitaka Omiya’s restaurant, with Shawn Joyeux running the room, at 5 rue de Poissy in the 5th, a short walk from Notre-Dame. Two Michelin stars as of the 2026 guide. The room is small, pale, and open. You can see the chefs through a glass window into the kitchen. Nobody is performing fine dining at you.

## What the room is like

The building is a normal Paris corner. Inside, the tables are close, the chairs are a soft green, and the lights are big rings. A glass pass looks straight into the kitchen, so you watch the plates being finished while you eat. It feels closer to a really good New York dining room than to the hushed version of a Paris star.

The servers match that. They were friendly, they joked, and they were not precious about any of it. That is rarer than the food at this level in Paris. You can talk to them like people. They also clearly like what they are serving. We ordered the seven-course menu and then more food kept arriving, not as a sales pitch, just because they wanted us to try it.

## Alliance menu and what it costs

The menus have names. Instant is lunch only, in three or four courses. Résonance is five. Harmonie is seven, which is the one we did, at lunch or dinner. They are carte blanche, built around what came in that day, and they will change a course if you need them to.

Prices move. When we went, a shorter lunch on the card in the window started around €95, and the longer menus were higher. Published figures for 2026 put Résonance near €175 and Harmonie near €245, before wine. Pairings were listed on that same window card, roughly €45 to €95 depending on the menu. Check [the current menus](https://www.restaurant-alliance.fr/en/a-la-carte-and-set-menus.html) before you book. Wine is Shawn Joyeux’s list, more than 700 bottles. The phone on the door is [+33 1 75 51 57 54](tel:+33175515754).

## What we actually ate

I do not have a printed name for every plate. This is what landed, and what it was.

Early on, a square of puff pastry with a thick layer of caviar on top. You eat it with your hands. Separately, a wide bowl of soft pale pieces, gnocchi or potato, under a cream sauce completely covered in caviar. One of those two is the best thing I have eaten. I would not argue if you called it the wagyu and caviar, or the caviar gnocchi. I just know I would book the restaurant again for that bite.

## Cheese, then the sweet things

Cheese is a choice, not a token plate. A black dish arrived with a long pale slice, a blue, a wedge with a rind, a soft piece, and a few smaller cuts, with white and rosé already on the table. They also walk you through the cheeses under glass. The one in front was a big blue, broken open, with the server pointing at what to take. It is a proper cheese course, and they enjoy talking about it.

The end of the meal was small and sweet, on the table rather than as one composed dessert. Two pale macarons on a wooden stand, a little tart with a white top and two green dots, a shell-shaped cake, and a tiny puff on a wavy plate. Rosé in the glass, and a server in a blue suit still leaning over the next table, which is about right for how the night felt.

## Is Alliance worth it?

Yes. If you want a Michelin restaurant in Paris and you do not want to be whispered at, this is the one I would send you to. The cooking is precise and not fussy. The room is cool without trying. The staff have a sense of humor. And they will feed you more than the menu you ordered if they are in a good mood, which they were.

Lunch and dinner are Monday to Friday. The restaurant’s site lists lunch from 12 to 2 and dinner from 7:30 to 11. Closed Saturday and Sunday. Book ahead. The [Michelin page](https://guide.michelin.com/en/ile-de-france/paris/restaurant/alliance) is here, the restaurant is [restaurant-alliance.fr](https://www.restaurant-alliance.fr/en/), and they post on [Instagram @restaurant_alliance](https://www.instagram.com/restaurant_alliance/).
