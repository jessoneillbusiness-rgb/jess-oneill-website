Skincare doesn't have to be complicated. After trying everything (and I mean *everything*), I've landed on a routine that's minimal, consistent, and actually works.

## The Philosophy

Less is more. Your skin is smart — it doesn't need twelve serums. It needs consistency, SPF, and patience.

## My AM Routine

1. **Gentle cleanser** — I love a cream cleanser that doesn't strip my skin
2. **Vitamin C serum** — Brightening and protective for the day ahead
3. **Lightweight moisturizer** — Something that layers well under makeup
4. **SPF 50** — Non-negotiable, every single day, even in winter

## My PM Routine

1. **Double cleanse** — Oil cleanser first, then gentle foam
2. **Retinol** (3x per week) — Started slow, built up over months
3. **Rich night cream** — NYC winters are brutal on skin
4. **Eye cream** — Because those late nights editing content catch up

## NYC-Specific Tips

- **Humidifier in winter** — Apartment heat is your skin's enemy
- **Blotting papers in summer** — Humidity is real
- **Facial mists on the subway** — A little luxury in transit

Beauty is self-care, not self-pressure. Find what works for *your* skin and stick with it.
