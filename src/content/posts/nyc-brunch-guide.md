New York City runs on brunch. It's the sacred weekend ritual — the slow morning, the perfect eggs, the people-watching that could fill a novel.

After years of exploring every borough, here are my absolute favorites.

## Manhattan Must-Tries

**Clinton St. Baking Company** — Their blueberry pancakes have a cult following for a reason. Go early or expect a wait, but it's worth every minute.

**Jack's Wife Freda** — Mediterranean-inspired brunch in a space that feels like your coolest friend's apartment. The shakshuka is legendary.

## Brooklyn Gems

**Sisters** — Clinton Hill neighborhood spot with a rotating menu and the kind of warm, unhurried vibe that makes you want to stay all afternoon.

**L'industrie Pizzeria** — Okay, technically pizza, but their morning slice with a cappuccino is a Brooklyn rite of passage.

## My Brunch Rules

1. **Never skip the coffee.** NYC coffee culture is half the experience.
2. **Share plates.** More dishes = more joy.
3. **Dress up a little.** Brunch is an occasion.
4. **Tip generously.** These servers are artists.
